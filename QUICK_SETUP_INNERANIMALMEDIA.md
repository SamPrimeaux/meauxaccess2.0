## 📋 Quick Setup (2 Minutes)

### Step 1: Open Workers Dashboard
**Click this link**:  
👉 https://dash.cloudflare.com/ede6590ac0d2fb7daf155b35653457b2/workers/services/view/iacess

### Step 2: Add Custom Domain
1. Click **"Triggers"** tab (top of page)
2. Scroll to **"Custom Domains"** section
3. Click **"Add Custom Domain"** button
4. Enter: `inneranimalmedia.com`
5. ✅ **Check "Override existing DNS records"** (if shown)
6. Click **"Add Custom Domain"**

### Step 3: Add www
1. Click **"Add Custom Domain"** again
2. Enter: `www.inneranimalmedia.com`
3. ✅ **Check "Override existing DNS records"** (if shown)
4. Click **"Add Custom Domain"**

**Done!** Cloudflare handles everything automatically.
